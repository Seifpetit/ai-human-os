1. PROJECT IN ONE BREATH
Gravity Ball is a competitive gravity-based ball game that should later support multiplayer.
Players do not control the ball directly. They influence it only by clicking on the court to create a gravity hole that pulls the ball.
Scoring comes from bending the ball into a goal, not from direct ball movement controls.

2. NEXT PLAYABLE WIN
One player can open the game in the browser, see a simple court with a moving ball, click somewhere on the court to create a visible gravity hole, and score by pulling the ball into a goal.
This pass should prove the core interaction and scoring loop clearly.

3. PLAYER PROMISE RIGHT NOW
- simple
- readable
- immediate to try
- satisfying physics influence
- focused on the core mechanic, not systems

4. MUST INCLUDE NOW
- a visible court and one visible ball
- click or tap on the court creates a visible gravity hole at that location
- the gravity hole clearly influences the ball's movement
- the gravity hole is the only way the player can influence the ball
- a simple goal and score update when the ball reaches it
- a clear reset or continue flow after a score so the loop can be repeated

5. MUST STAY OUT NOW
- real multiplayer networking
- rooms, matchmaking, or lobby systems
- server sync and latency handling
- chat, accounts, profiles, or progression
- cosmetic polish beyond what is needed to read the mechanic
- multiple game modes
- multiple balls
- advanced menus or settings

6. BUDGET AND STAGE
Project Stage:
prototype

Feature Budget:
1

Complexity Budget:
Keep this to one clean mechanic slice: one court, one ball, one scoring rule, and one player-visible interaction.
Actively avoid network architecture, broad game flow, deep UI systems, or anything that does not directly prove click -> gravity hole -> ball influence -> score.

7. HARD CONSTRAINTS
- browser-first
- the player must never drag, push, or directly move the ball
- no keyboard control as the main mechanic
- clicking or tapping to create a gravity hole is the only control input for influencing the ball
- scoring must come from where the ball ends up on the court
- keep the implementation simple enough to validate the loop quickly
- do not introduce unrelated systems just because the long-term game will be multiplayer
- choices made now should not block a later server-authoritative multiplayer version

8. FUTURE, BUT NOT NOW
- real multiplayer play with multiple people affecting the same ball
- server-authoritative physics and sync
- conflict resolution between multiple simultaneous gravity holes from different players
- room creation and joining
- better scoring rules, rounds, and match structure
- balancing, polish, audiovisual juice, and progression

9. OPTIONAL NOTES OR CORRECTIONS
The core identity of the game is that the player influences the ball indirectly by placing gravity holes on the court.
Do not reinterpret the game as direct ball control, paddle control, character control, or a standard sports game.
If a simplification is needed, simplify everything except that core rule.
